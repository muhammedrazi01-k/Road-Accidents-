# 🚦 Road Traffic Accidents Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Road Traffic Accidents Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive data visualization project developed to analyze road traffic accidents based on geographical areas, driver age, road surface conditions, collision types, driving experience, and weather conditions.

The dashboard provides meaningful insights into road safety patterns and accident-related factors through interactive visualizations and filters.

This project aims to identify accident-prone areas, understand driver demographics, evaluate environmental factors, and analyze collision patterns to support better road safety planning and decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze the total number of people involved in road traffic accidents.
- Identify accident-prone geographical areas.
- Understand accident distribution across different driver age groups.
- Analyze accidents based on road surface conditions.
- Identify the most common collision types.
- Evaluate the relationship between driving experience and accidents.
- Understand the impact of weather conditions on road accidents.
- Analyze accident patterns based on vehicle type and driver gender.

## 📊 Dashboard Highlights

### 1. Total Number of People

- Displays the total number of people represented in the accident dataset.
- Total people: **7,544**.
- Provides an overview of the accident-related records represented in the dashboard.

### 2. Vehicle Type Filter

Allows users to filter the dashboard based on different vehicle types.

This feature helps analyze accident patterns associated with specific vehicle categories.

### 3. Sex of Driver Filter

Users can interactively filter accident data based on driver gender:

- Female
- Male
- Unknown

This helps understand accident distribution across different driver groups.

### 4. Accident by Area

A column chart visualizes the distribution of accident-related records across different geographical areas.

| Area | Number of Vehicles Involved |
|---|---:|
| Church Areas | 1,401 |
| Hospital Areas | 172 |
| Industrial Areas | 574 |
| Market Areas | 89 |
| Office Areas | 4,307 |
| Outside Rural Areas | 270 |
| Recreational Areas | 408 |
| Residential Areas | 2,594 |
| Rural Village Areas | 55 |
| Rural Village (Other) | 23 |
| School Areas | 522 |

**Key Insight:** Office areas have the highest displayed count, with 4,307, followed by residential areas with 2,594.

This indicates a significant concentration of accident-related activity in office and residential locations.

### 5. Accident by Age

This chart analyzes the distribution of vehicles involved across different driver age groups.

| Age Group | Vehicles Involved |
|---|---:|
| 18–30 | 6,130 |
| 31–50 | 5,805 |
| Over 51 | 2,256 |
| Under 18 | 1,193 |

**Key Insight:**
- The 18–30 age group has the highest displayed count.
- The 31–50 age group also represents a significant proportion.
- Drivers under 18 have the lowest displayed count.

This analysis helps identify age groups that require greater road safety awareness.

### 6. Accident by Road Surface

A pie chart represents accident distribution according to road surface conditions.

| Road Surface | Percentage |
|---|---:|
| Dry | 74.56% |
| Wet or Damp | 24.68% |
| Snow | 0.74% |

**Key Insight:** Most recorded accidents occurred on dry road surfaces, accounting for 74.56% of the displayed distribution.

Wet or damp road conditions account for 24.68%, highlighting the importance of safe driving practices during unfavorable road conditions.

### 7. Accidents by Collision Type

A treemap visualization displays the distribution of accidents across different collision types.

Major collision categories include:

- Vehicle with Vehicle Collision
- Collision with Roadside Objects
- Collision with Other Objects
- Rollover

**Key Insight:** Vehicle-to-vehicle collisions represent the largest displayed category, with 11,089 records.

Collision with roadside objects accounts for 2,293 records, while rollover accidents account for 535 records.

This visualization helps identify common accident types and understand collision patterns.

### 8. Accidents by Driving Experience

A bubble chart visualizes accident distribution across different driving experience levels.

Experience categories include:

- Below 1 Year
- 1–2 Years
- 2–5 Years
- 5–10 Years
- Above 10 Years

**Key Insight:** The visualization helps compare accident-related records across different driving experience groups.

It can support the identification of experience levels that may require additional driver training and road safety awareness.

### 9. Accidents by Weather Condition

A pie chart analyzes accident distribution based on weather conditions.

| Weather Condition | Percentage |
|---|---:|
| Normal | 86.19% |
| Raining | 11.49% |
| Other | 2.32% |

**Key Insight:** Most displayed accidents occurred under normal weather conditions, accounting for 86.19%.

Rainy conditions represent 11.49% of the distribution, demonstrating the importance of maintaining safe driving habits in different weather conditions.
